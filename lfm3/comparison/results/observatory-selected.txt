The observatory has one public telescope and a narrow staircase leading to the dome. On a clear evening visitors often reach the top faster than the telescope operator can show them an object. If the next group waits on the stairs, people leaving the dome have very little space to pass. The problem is the position of the queue as much as its length.

For the next two open evenings, visitors will collect a numbered card in the downstairs hall. A volunteer will call four numbers at a time and wait for the previous group to come down before sending the next group up. Visitors who miss their first call can speak to the volunteer and join a later group. The cards are intended to organise the queue, so they will be issued without names or telephone numbers.

A small monitor in the hall will show which numbers are in the dome. It will also carry a short description of the object currently in the telescope. If cloud covers the object for more than ten minutes, the operator will pause the calls and give an update over the hall speaker. After the second evening the volunteers will compare the longest waiting time with the time recorded at the last open evening. They will also note whether the staircase stayed clear.
