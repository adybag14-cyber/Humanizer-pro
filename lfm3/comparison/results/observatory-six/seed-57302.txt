The observatory has one public telescope and a narrow staircase to the dome. On a clear evening, visitors often get there faster than the telescope operator can show an object. If a group waits on the stairs, people leaving the dome have little room to pass. The real problem is the queue’s position, not just its length.

For the next two open evenings, visitors collect a numbered card in the downstairs hall. A volunteer calls four numbers at a time and waits for the previous group to go down before sending the next group up. Missed first calls let visitors talk to the volunteer and join a later group. The cards organize the queue, so they are given without names or phone numbers.

A small monitor in the hall shows which numbers are in the dome and gives a short description of the current object. If cloud covers the object for more than ten minutes, the operator pauses the calls and reads an update over the hall speaker. After the second evening, volunteers compare the longest waiting time with the last open evening’s record and note whether the staircase stayed clear.
