The observatory has one public telescope and a narrow staircase leading to the dome; on a clear evening visitors often outpace the telescope operator reaching the top. If a group waits on the stairs, those leaving the dome have little space to pass, making both the queue’s position and its length important.

To fix this, for the next two open evenings visitors receive a numbered card in the downstairs hall. A volunteer calls four numbers at a time and waits for the previous group to descend before sending the next call. Missed first calls allow visitors to meet a later group by speaking to the volunteer. The cards organize the queue but are given without names or phone numbers.

A small monitor in the hall displays which numbers correspond to objects currently in the dome and shows a brief description of the telescope target. If cloud cover lasts more than ten minutes, the operator pauses the calls and announces an update over the hall speaker. After the second evening, volunteers will compare the longest waiting time with the previous open‑evening record and note whether the staircase remained clear.
